About the fest

Where tradition flows into today

Pravah — meaning "flow" — is our college's annual fest, bringing together the color of Rajasthani culture with the energy of a modern campus. It's nine days of performance, music, and competition, built by students, for students.

We blend cultural showcases with technical and gaming events so every kind of talent has a stage: dancers and coders, singers and quizzers, designers and gamers all under one roof.

Pravah exists to give you a space to showcase what you're good at, discover what you didn't know you could do, and have a genuinely good time doing it.
